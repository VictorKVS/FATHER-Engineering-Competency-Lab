# G7 K2 — Legal / Access + Provenance Qualification

K2 qualifies the second Knowledge Factory production block.

Acceptance:
- Ruff/static correctness;
- full father-chassis regression;
- explicitly allowed source + required evidence -> APPROVED;
- denied rights -> REJECTED;
- unknown/ambiguous rights -> HOLD;
- missing evidence -> HOLD;
- source without content hash -> HOLD;
- provenance binds exact source id/hash/locator/version/decision;
- source hash substitution detected;
- policy id/version preserved;
- qualification artifact contains metadata/evidence refs only, not raw source content.

K2 does not qualify parsing, chunking, malware/MIME validation or jurisdiction-specific legal packs.
