# Business Process
Owner: Senior Business Analyst.

Goal:
prevent an agent from performing work solely because it has the correct role name.

Business rule:
ROLE permits a category of work.
COMPETENCY proves ability to perform that work at the required level.

Input:
workflow stage, assigned agent, competency claim, competency evidence, stage competency policy.

Output:
ADMIT or BLOCK with reasons and evidence references.

KPIs:
- under-qualified agent admitted: 0%;
- missing evidence admitted: 0%;
- unknown competency level admitted: 0%;
- admission decisions traceable to evidence: 100%.

Value:
turns role-based dispatch into evidence-based professional admission.
