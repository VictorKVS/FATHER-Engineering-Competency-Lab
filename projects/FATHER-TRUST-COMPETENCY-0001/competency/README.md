# Competency Engine

The same evidence engine evaluates PERSON and PRODUCT subjects.

## Claim model

```json
{
  "claim_id": "CLM-...",
  "subject_id": "SUBJECT-...",
  "subject_scope": "person",
  "domain": "AI_ARCHITECTURE",
  "capability": "design_model_routing_with_failover",
  "claimed_level": "C4",
  "required_evidence": [],
  "status": "UNQUALIFIED"
}
```

## Levels

- C0 CLAIMED
- C1 LEARNED
- C2 DEMONSTRATED
- C3 REPEATABLE
- C4 INTEGRATED
- C5 QUALIFIED
- C6 INDUSTRIAL / PROFESSIONAL

## Person example

A course completion may support C1.
A reproducible lab may support C2.
Repeated independent implementation may support C3.
Integration into a real architecture may support C4.
Externally or internally qualified evidence may support C5.
Sustained professional evidence across contexts may support C6.

## Product example

A feature declaration is C0.
A unit demonstration may support C2.
Repeated integration tests may support C3/C4.
A qualification gate with signed evidence may support C5.
Industrial qualification with lifecycle evidence may support C6.

No automatic promotion is allowed without policy-defined evidence.
