# Interview & Survey System

## Interview types
- sponsor interview
- stakeholder interview
- user interview
- expert interview
- lost-user interview
- current-process interview
- buyer interview
- operator/support interview

## Interview blocks
1. Context
2. Current behavior
3. Problems
4. Frequency
5. Severity
6. Workarounds
7. Cost of current process
8. Decision criteria
9. Risks
10. Adoption barriers
11. Budget / willingness to pay
12. Success definition

## Survey rules
- avoid leading questions
- separate behavior from opinion
- use time-bounded questions
- ask about current actions before hypothetical intent
- record sample limitations
- record response bias
- do not generalize beyond evidence

## Evidence levels
E0 opinion
E1 single anecdote
E2 repeated pattern
E3 measured behavior
E4 market/operational data
E5 validated experiment
