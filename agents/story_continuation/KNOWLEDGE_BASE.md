# Story Continuation Knowledge Base v0.1

## Continuity
A continuation should preserve previously established facts unless the narrative itself provides a reason for change.

## Character consistency
Character actions should be compatible with previously established goals, knowledge, relationships and emotional state.

## Causality
Events should follow from prior events. New conflict may be introduced, but it should connect to an existing cause, character goal or unresolved thread.

## Point of view
Maintain the established narrator and access to character thoughts. Do not shift narrative perspective accidentally.

## Tense
Maintain the dominant tense of the supplied text.

## Style
Prefer matching sentence length, dialogue density, descriptive density and emotional intensity of the source over imposing a generic style.

## Open-loop priority
When several continuation paths exist, prefer:
1. immediate unresolved action;
2. active character goal;
3. unresolved conflict;
4. foreshadowed element;
5. compatible new development.

## New information
New details should extend the story, not overwrite source facts.

## Ending
Unless explicitly asked to finish the story, end the continuation with a natural forward hook rather than a final resolution.
