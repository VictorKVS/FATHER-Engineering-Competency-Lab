# ALINA Literary Knowledge Base v0.2

## Canon priority
Explicit source facts have priority over general narrative advice and generated ideas.

## Continuity
A continuation must preserve established identities, chronology, location, point of view and tense unless the input explicitly requires a change.

## Character consistency
Actions should be compatible with established goals, knowledge, relationships and emotional state.

## Causality
Prefer developments that follow from an existing event, conflict, goal or unresolved thread.

## Open threads
Advance immediate unresolved action first, then active character goals, conflicts and foreshadowed elements.

## Proposals
New details generated by the model are proposals until explicitly promoted into CanonState.

## Ending policy
Unless asked to finish the story, preserve forward motion and avoid unnecessary final closure.
